# Mechanic

## Incantation Syntony

To trigger Incantation Syntony you need to use at least 3 cards of the **same** types

For example:

<div class="flex gap-4 items-center">
  <img src="/data/card/attr-attack.png" alt="attr-attack" />
  <span class="text-4xl">+</span>
  <img src="/data/card/attr-attack.png" alt="attr-attack" />
  <span class="text-4xl">+</span>
  <img src="/data/card/attr-attack.png" alt="attr-attack" />
</div>

By playing 3 Attack cards, we will trigger Attack Syntony Lv.1

#### Levels

- <span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 1:</span> **3** same types of Incantation
- <span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 2:</span> **4** same types of Incantation
- <span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 3:</span> **5** same types of Incantation

## Incantation Resonance

To trigger Incantation Resonance you need to use at least 3 cards of the **different** types

#### Example

<div class="flex gap-4 items-center">
  <img src="/data/card/attr-attack.png" alt="attr-attack" />
  <span class="text-4xl">+</span>
  <img src="/data/card/attr-buff.png" alt="attr-buff" />
  <span class="text-4xl">+</span>
  <img src="/data/card/attr-debuff.png" alt="attr-debuff" />
</div>

By playing 3 different types of cards, we will trigger Resonance Lv.1

#### Levels

- <span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 1:</span> **3** different types of Incantation
- <span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 2:</span> **4** different types of Incantation
- <span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 3:</span> **5** different types of Incantation
