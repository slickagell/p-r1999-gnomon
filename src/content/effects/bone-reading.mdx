code: "BONE_READING"
type: "UNKNOWN"

When this Channel status is active at the end of the round, consume 2 Eureka to cast incantation :effect{code="ADMONITION"} (if there isn't enough Eureka, all Eureka is used up. If Eureka is 0 after casting, this status is removed).
