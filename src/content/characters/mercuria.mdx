## Portray

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 1:</span>
_Lengthy Journey_: At the end of the round, now if allies have casted two or more rank 2 incantations for that round, gain Moxie +1 for self.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 2:</span>
_Lengthy Journey_: Critical DMG increase is now **+50%** and Incantation Might increase is now **+30%**.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 3:</span>
_Lengthy Journey_: :effect{code="COSMIC_ENERGY"}'s stack limit is increased to **30**. The DMG Dealt granted by each stack is increased to **+2.5%**.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 4:</span>
_Morning Star for the Night_: Mental DMG is increased to **475%**, and extends :effect{code="RANK_UP"} by +1 round.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 5:</span>
_Lengthy Journey_: Extends :effect{code="COSMIC_ENERGY"} by +1 round. The DMG Dealt granted by each stack is increased to **+3%**.
