## Inheritance

### Psychoanalysis

<div class="mt-4 flex items-center">
  <InsightImg classNames="w-8 mr-2" insight="1" />

  ##### Insight 1
</div>

When attacked, directs **50%** of the damage taken by other allies to Kakania instead. Whenever Kakania takes damage, **10%** of that damage is stored as :effect{code="EMPATHY"}, which can store up to **20%** of Kakania's Max HP. Once :effect{code="EMPATHY"} is full, Kakania can no longer take damage on behalf of other allies.

  <InsightImg classNames="w-8 mr-2" insight="2" />

<div class="mt-4 flex items-center">
  <InsightImg classNames="w-8 mr-2" insight="3" />

  ##### Insight 3
</div>

Whenever :effect{code="EMPATHY"} stores (Kakania's Max HP x**3%**) damage, recovers own (Max HP x**10%**) HP for all allies. When being healed, deals (current :effect{code="EMPATHY"} x**100%**) Genesis DMG to all enemies.

## Portray

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 1:</span>
*Association*: :effect{code="SOLACE"}'s ATK bonus is increased to **400**, and the additional ATK bonus based on :effect{code="EMPATHY"} value is increased to **15%**.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 2:</span>
*Psychoanalysis*: The threshold for :effect{code="EMPATHY"} to trigger healing is decreased from **5%** HP to **2%** HP. When healed, the Genesis DMG dealt is increased to (Current :effect{code="EMPATHY"} value x**120%**).

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 3:</span>
*Psychoanalysis*: :effect{code="EMPATHY"}'s maximum value is increased to **30%** of Max HP. The percentage of damage taken that's converted to :effect{code="EMPATHY"} is changed to **15%**.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 4:</span>
*Subconscious*: At rank **1/2/3**, the Genesis DMG dealt is increased to (current :effect{code="EMPATHY"} value x**270/310/350%**).

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 5:</span>
*Id, Ego, and Superego*: The additional Genesis DMG dealt is increased to (current :effect{code="EMPATHY"} value x**1300%**); the Genesis DMG dealt at the end of the round is increased to (recorded value x**45%**).
