## Portray

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 1:</span>
*Baijiu and Mayham*: Reality DMG dealt is increased to **400%**. Extra Reality DMG dealt is increased to **+150%** for each missing enemy (up to **+300%**).

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 2:</span>
*Bottoms Up*: For each stack of :effect{code="LIQUOR"} consumed, Reality DMG dealt is increased to **+90%**.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 3:</span>
*Contemplation*: Random :effect{code="COLLECTION_OF_BUFFS"} effect granted to self is increased to 2 types.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 4:</span>
*Bottoms Up*: Maximum stacks of :effect{code="LIQUOR"} consumed is increased to 4, and chance to consume **1/2/3/4** stacks of :effect{code="LIQUOR"} is increased to **5%/15%/40%/40%**.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 5:</span>
*Contemplation*: :effect{code="COLLECTION_OF_BUFFS"}'s effect are now increased to **+25%** each. At the end of the round, now for for every 3 different types of statuses possessed, grant 1 stack of :effect{code="LIQUOR"} to self (now gain up to 5 stacks).
