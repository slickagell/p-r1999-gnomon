## Portray

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 1:</span>
*The First Push*: *\[Conservation of Energy]*'s Reality DMG is increased to **150%**; now. deals additional damage when the target's current HP is below **75%**, and the additional DMG is increased to **100%**.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 2:</span>
*A Step Forward*: :effect{code="PRAGMATIST"} and :effect{code="LEADERSHIP"}'s durations are increased to 3 rounds, and the Stronger Afflatus Damage Factor increase is now **20%**.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 3:</span>
*The First Push*: :effect{code="DATA_ITERATION"}'s ATK bonus is increased to **6%**; and whenever an enemy dies, the stacks of :effect{code="DATA_ITERATION"} gained is increased to **2**.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 4:</span>
*The First Push*: *\[Conservation of Energy]*'s Reality DMG is increased to **200%**; the maximum number of times *\[Conservation of Energy]* can trigger when enemies die is increased to **4** times per round.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 5:</span>
*The First Push*: :effect{code="DATA_ITERATION"}'s ATK bonus is increased to **8%**, and maximum stacks is increased to **6**; and whenever an ally gains 1 Moxie or Eureka, the stacks of :effect{code="DYNAMO"} gained is increased to **2**.
