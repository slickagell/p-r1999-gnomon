## Inheritance

### Fungus Ecosystem Machine

<div class="mt-4 flex items-center">
  <InsightImg classNames="w-8 mr-2" insight="1" />

  ##### Insight 1
</div>

When entering battle, gain Eureka +2. After casting an Ultimate, gain Eureka +4. When casting a normal incantation, if mushrooms are maxed out and match the incantation type, spend 2 Eureka to grow the matching mushrooms 1 time and trigger them if they are mature.

<div class="mt-4 flex items-center">
  <InsightImg classNames="w-8 mr-2" insight="2" />

  ##### Insight 2
</div>

DMG Taken **-8%** when the caster enters battle.

<div class="mt-4 flex items-center">
  <InsightImg classNames="w-8 mr-2" insight="3" />

  ##### Insight 3
</div>

At the end of the round, for every 1 mushroom on the field, recover (own Missing HP x**8%**) HP. This effect can critically hit. After casting a basic incantation, if the mushrooms are maxed out but don't match the incantation type, spend 2 Eureka to gain Moxie +1 (only triggers when Moxie is below 5).

## Portray

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 1:</span>
*Fungus Ecosystem Machine*: When entering battle, additional Eureka **+2**; Each mushroom's recovery amount is increased to **12%** of own Lost HP.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 2:</span>
*Protection to the Body*: At Incantation rank 1/2, the HP loss is reduced to **10%** of current HP. The :effect{code="VULNERABILITY"} inflicted by :effect{code="HONEY_FUNGUS"} increases to 3 stacks.

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 3:</span>
*Stimulus to the Mind*: At Incantation rank 1/2/3, the :effect{code="SHIELD"} granted to all allies is increased to (Own HP x**20%/25%/35%**).

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 4:</span>
*Protection to the Body*: At Incantation rank 1/2/3, the inflicted Genesis DMG is increased to (Own HP x**20%/25%/35%**).

<span class="text-2xl mr-2">Lv. 5:</span>
*Stimulus to the Mind*: At Incantation rank 1/2, the HP loss is reduced to 10% of current HP. The :effect{code="DISCERNMENT"} granted by :effect{code="GHOST_FUNGUS"} increases to 3 stacks.
