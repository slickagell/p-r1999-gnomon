Initial Lineup Clamor Cap 20

## Catalyst Effects

* Gain Vibrant Quill when starting out. Initial Lineup Clamor Cap +5. Only Support type disks will drop.
* At the start of the round, generates a rank 1 Precast Aid incantation for arandom ally. When an ally casts an Aid incantation, gain Moxie +1 for self.

![image-4](/images/catalysts/incantation_icon_4.png)

![image-5](/images/catalysts/incantation_icon_5.png)
