# Euphotic Zone 20M

### Recommended

- Plant units
- Insight II Level 42

### Stage mechanism

:text[[All]]{className="font-medium text-all"} Stronger Afflatus Damage **+10%**. When being attacked by a non-stronger Afflatus, Final DMG Taken Reduction **+5%**. After round 15 in battle, all monsters gain the Frenzy status.

:text[[Enemy]]{className="font-medium text-enemy"} Reality DEF **+10%**, Mental DEF **+10%**.
