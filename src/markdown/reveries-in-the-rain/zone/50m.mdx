# Euphotic Zone 50M

### Recommended

- Beast units
- Insight II Level 45

### Stage mechanism

:text[[All]]{className="font-medium text-all"} Stronger Afflatus Damage **+10%**. When being attacked by a non-stronger Afflatus, Final DMG Taken Reduction **+5%**. After round 15 in battle, all monsters gain the Frenzy status.

:text[[Ally]]{className="font-medium text-ally"} When the Banking Capitalist is being attacked, 5 Goldfinch Coins are stolen from him. Stealing a sufficient amount of Goldfinch Coins will also lead to victory.
