    * Moldir's Ultimate will grant herself Critical Rate **+100%** and Critical DMG **+50%** for **3** rounds.
    * There are enemies call *Shieldbearer* that will be in :effect{code="TAUNT"} status to protect *Moldir* from being attacked.

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-title">
    ## Strategy and tips
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-content">
    * Prioritize **Beast** or **single-target** units.
    * You should bring units with **dispel** skills to dispel *\[Pos Status]* and *\[Counter]* from enemies.
    * You can deal with *Shieldbearer* first to remove them from protecting *Moldir*.
    * You can choose units have skills that are **not** *\[Attack]* type skills and let them have the highest ATK to not be affected by :effect{code="DISARM"}.
    * Characters like *Lopera* should not be used to deal with *Moldir* since they are affected by :effect{code="DISARM"}, which will cause them to lose ability to perform their Follow-up attacks.
    * The **best** Myth Manifest to use is *Operatic Reflection* since it has stronger Afflatus, provides effects to *Beast* units.
