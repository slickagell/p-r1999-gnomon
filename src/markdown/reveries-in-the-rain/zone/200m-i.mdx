  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="stage-info-content">
    - There are *Moldir* and enemies, you just need to defeat *Moldir* to pass the stage.
    - At the end of each round, *Moldir* will perform an additional attack to the highest ATK ally and make that ally fall into :effect{code="DISARM"} status.
    - When being attacked in *[Counter]* status, *Moldir* will riposte and also inflict :effect{code="DISARM"} on the attacker.
    - There are enemies call *Shieldbearer* that will be in :effect{code="TAUNT"} status to protect *Moldir* from being attacked.

  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-title">
    ## Strategy and tips
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-content">
    - Focus on dealing with *Moldir*.
    - You can deal with *Shieldbearer* first to remove them from protecting *Moldir*.
    - Prioritize **Beast** or **single-target** units.
    - You can choose units have skills that are **not** *[Attack]* type skills and let them have the highest ATK to not be affected by :effect{code="DISARM"}.
    - Characters like *Lopera* or *Joe* should not be used to deal with *Moldir* since they are affected by :effect{code="DISARM"}, which will cause them to lose ability to perform their Follow-up attacks.
    - The **best** Myth Manifest to use is *Operatic Reflection* since it has stronger Afflatus, provides effects to *Beast* units and at the beginning of each round, perform an attack that can reduce enemies's :effect{code="DODGE"} stacks.
  </Fragment>
