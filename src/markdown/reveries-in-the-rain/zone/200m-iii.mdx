  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-title">
    ## Strategy and tips
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-content">
    * Make sure HP of all allies in team is around **70-80%** to be able to withstand the boss's attacks

    * **Attack** and **Channel** incantations are recommended to use more often, since it will not grant *Spathodea* :effect{code="UNSPARING"} or :effect{code="WEAL"} stacks.

    * Bring units with :effect{param="seal"} or :effect{param="nightmare"} skills to prevent *Spathodea* from casting Ultimate.
      * Especially, try to use it at **75%/50%/25%** *Spathodea*'s HP
      * Do **not** use these effect skills twice in a row, since *Spathodea* has *\[Control Resilience]* 100%, which means she will not be affected by these effects for upcoming rounds, for example: if :effect{param="seal"} takes effect for 3 rounds, means *Spathodea* will not be affected by :effect{param="seal"} for the next 3 rounds after :effect{param="seal"} ends.
      * **Caution** for :effect{param="nightmare"} effect: since this effect will be dispelled when taking any attack, it is recommended to use it at the **end** of Spell queue and do **not** team up with characters or Myth Manifest that has Follow-up attacks at the end of round.

    * Another way to prevent Ultimate is choosing characters have **reduce Moxie** skills

    * With Myth Manifest:
      * Choose *Mountain Ghost* to go with *\[Poison]* or *\[Rank Up]* team
      * *Star of Misfortune* can be use to go with *\[Star]* unit team, but **limit** the use of Ultimate because it is a *Buff* skill, which can grant boss :effect{code="UNSPARING"} stacks.
      * *Operatic Reflection* is **not** recommended since it will cast a Debuff skill each round, which will grant *Spathodea* lots of :effect{code="WEAL"} stacks.
  </Fragment>
