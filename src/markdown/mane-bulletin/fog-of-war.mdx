    ## Stage mechanism
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="stage-mechanism-content">
    * For both allies and enemies, after taking action, gain **5** stacks of :effect{param="schemed"} and :effect{param="fading-schemed"}.

    * **Mental DMG** units gain **20%** stats increase in ATK, DEF and HP

    * After casting a **Counter** or **Channel** incantation, gain Moxie **+1** for self (only once per round for each character).

    * When another enemy takes action, FoW gain **1** stack of :effect{param="charged-munitions"}.

    * When another enemy get a *\[Control]* status, FoW gain **2** stacks of :effect{param="charged-munitions"}.

    * At the end of the round, for every **2** stacks of :effect{param="charged-munitions"}, FoW will cast skill *\[Collateral Impact]* **1** time that deals Reality DMG, if there is no *\[Neg Status]* on self, deals **+30%** Reality DMG.

    * When FoW use Ultimate, for every stacks of :effect{param="charged-munitions"}, increase Reality DMG Dealt by **100%**.

    * At the end of the round, if FoW has more than **10** stacks of :effect{param="charged-munitions"}, remove all stacks of :effect{param="charged-munitions"} and gain **4** stacks of :effect{param="broken"}.

    * Every time FoW attack, it gains **1** stack of buff that increases DMG Taken Reduction by **50%** (stackable).

    * There are 2 believer that buff :effect{param="force-field"} effects that increase DMG Taken Reduction by **50%** to all enemies when not in *\[Control]* status.
      * *Zealous Believer* (male) will buff DMG Taken Reduction by **Mental** DMG.
      * *Devout Believer* (female) will buff DMG Taken Reduction by **Reality** DMG.
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-title">
    ## Strategy and tips
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-content">
    * Prioritize **Mental DMG** units since they deal more damage.

    * Use units with *\[Control]* skills to inflict *\[Control]* on Believers.

    * Focus on dealing with *Zealous Believer* first to dispel Mental Barrier then attack FoW.

    * Use Follow-up attacks to deal with Believers and :effect{param="charged-munitions"} stacks of FoW.
  </Fragment>
