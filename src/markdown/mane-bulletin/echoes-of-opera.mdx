  <Fragment slot="stage-mechanism-title">
    ## Stage mechanism
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="stage-mechanism-content">
    * The main boss is *Misplaced Artist*.

    * All units gain **20%** stats increase in ATK, DEF and HP.

    * After each action, gain **10%** DMG Bonus and Leech Rate **5%**.

    * Upon entering battle, mark character at the leftmost position a *\[Special Guest]* status, allow to deal more DMG to *Misplaced Artist* and gain **10** stack of :effect{param="heightened-standards"}.

    * When an enemy is defeated, dispel **2** :effect{param="heightened-standards"} stacks from the *\[Special Guest]*.

    * When the *\[Special Guest]* is received **Health** or **Buff** incantation
      * If it's a **1-target** incantation, dispel **2** :effect{param="heightened-standards"} stacks.
      * If it's **AoE** incantation, dispel **1** :effect{param="heightened-standards"} stack.

    * At the end of the round, *Misplaced Artist* deal Mental DMG to all our allies and addition Generis DMG to the *\[Special Guest]*, if the *\[Special Guest]* attacked it this round, it will also inflict **4** stacks of :effect{param="heightened-standards"}.

    * If :effect{param="heightened-standards"} stacks less than **5** stacks, *\[Special Guest]* will gain DMG Dealt **+50%**, Crit DMG **+50%** and Penetration Rate **+50%**.

    #### Crisis Column mode

    * At the start of each round, enemies gain a buff that increases DMG Dealt by **10%**, when they're defeated, dispel all stacks from their other allies.
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-title">
    ## Strategy and tips
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-content">
    * Select 1 character **with Burst DMG** skills and position them at the leftmost position.

    * With other positions, favor buffers and healers since they dealt **30%** less DMG.

    * Try to decrease :effect{param="heightened-standards"} stacks below **5** to deal more DMG to *Misplaced Artist*.
  </Fragment>
