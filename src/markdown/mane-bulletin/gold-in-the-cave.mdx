    * After using Ultimate, all allies will increase their DMG Dealt **30%** for **1** round, and heal the ally with the lowest HP with **10%** of Max HP.

    * Before being hit by Ultimate, GitC will lose all its unique buffs. When it is hit by Ultimate, DMG Taken **+30%**.

    * Every **2** rounds, it will inflict :effect{code="DISARM"} on the unit with the highest ATK for **1** round.

    * If any of GitC's unique reaches **5** stack, the duration of :effect{code="DISARM"} effect will be doubled, last for **2** rounds.

    * Its ultimate will dispel all *\[Stats Up]* on our team and inflict :effect{code="DISARM"} and :effect{code="SILENCE"} for **2** rounds.

    #### Crisis Column mode

    * GitC applies a debuff to our allies at the end of the round that reduces Incantation Might by **10%**.
