    ## Stage mechanism
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="stage-mechanism-content">
    * The main boss is *Strange Doctor*, score is earned by attacking it.

    * **Beast** and **Plant** afflatus units gain **20%** stats increase in ATK, DEF and HP

    * Our allies after cast a non-attack skill will gain **1** :effect{param="rousing-morale"} stack and Ultimate will gain **2** :effect{param="rousing-morale"} stacks.

    * At the start of each round, *Strange Doctor* will enter :effect{code="MASTERMIND"} status.
      * If *Futurism* is in *\[Control]* status, exit that status, gain *Futurism* **3** :effect{param="dynamo"} stack and suffer :effect{code="TRUE_FORM_EXPOSED"} for **1** round.

    * If all robots are dead, *Strange Doctor* will suffer :effect{code="TRUE_FORM_EXPOSED"} for **3** rounds.

    * *Strange Doctor* rotation:
      * First, it will buff DMG bonus to all enemies and gain *Futurism* **1** :effect{param="dynamo"} stack and **+1** Moxie.
      * Second, it will attack **2** units with the highest HP percentages, inflict Ultimate Might **-15%** for **1** round, if there's no Test robot **2** on the field, summon it (up to 4 enemies on field).
      * Repeat the above two steps.
      * Ultimate will gain a Shield for **2** rounds, enter prepare status for **2** rounds then dispel all *\[Neg Status]* on self, gain *Futurism* with **2** :effect{param="dynamo"} stack, DMG bonus **+50%** and **+5** Moxie to all enemies.

    * *Futurism* mechanism:
      * Enters battle, gain **4** :effect{param="dynamo"} stacks.
      * At the end of each turn or being attacked, lose **1** :effect{param="dynamo"} stack.
      * If lose all :effect{param="dynamo"} stacks, enter :effect{code="STAGGERED"} status.
      * When in :effect{param="burn"}, :effect{param="poison"} and :effect{param="corrode"} status, DMG Dealt **-25%**.
      * Every turn, it attacks **2** units with the highest HP percentages.

    * Test robots **1** and **2** have DMG taken fixed at **1**, and has total HP of **8** and **3**.

    #### Crisis Column mode

    * If any enemies use Ultimates, gain **2** :effect{param="sturdiness"} stacks to all enemies.
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-title">
    ## Strategy and tips
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-content">
    * Prioritize **Beast** & **Plant** units since they deal more damage.

    * Focus on dealing with *Futurism* and *Strange Doctor*.

    * When *Futurism* is not in :effect{code="STAGGERED"} status, the *Strange Doctor* will takes masively reduce Reality DMG, so focus on dealing with *Futurism* until he is in :effect{code="STAGGERED"}, then focus on dealing with the *Strange Doctor*.

    * Inflict DoT effects like :effect{param="burn"}, :effect{param="poison"} and :effect{param="corrode"} will reduce the DMG dealt by *Futurism* and deals DMG to other test robots.
  </Fragment>
