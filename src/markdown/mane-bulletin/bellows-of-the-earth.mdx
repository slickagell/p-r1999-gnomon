    ## Stage mechanism
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="stage-mechanism-content">
    * For both allies and enemies, whenever is attacked, reduces the DMG taken by **5%** for that turn (stackable up to **5** times).

    * When allies defeat an enemy, DMG Bonus **+1%** (stackable up to 20 times). After **10** stacks, allies also gain **10%** Penetration Rate.

    * When allies use a mass attack skill, gains DMG Bonus **+30%** and Leech Rate **+10%**.

    * When a minion is defeated, gains all enemies 2 *\[Build Up]* stacks, and BotE will trigger :tooltip[Mass attack. Deals **50%** Reality DMG to all its allies and and additional DMG equal to **10%** Max HP.]{title="[Energy Explosion]"}.

    * For each minions alive, BotE gains Penetration Rate **+10%**.

    * At the end of the round, BotE gains a buff that increases DMG Bonus **5%** (stacks up to **10** times), remove **1** stack when being attacked.

    * At the end of the round, if there are less than **3** minions alive, BotE will consume **6%** of HP left and summon a new minion.

    * Both BotE and minions when being attacked, will gain **1** *\[Build Up]* stack, if it's a active action, it will gain additional **2** stacks. Upon reaching **9** stacks, BotE will trigger :tooltip[Mass attack. Deals **50%** Reality DMG to all its allies and and additional DMG equal to **10%** Max HP.]{title="[Energy Explosion]"}.
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-title">
    ## Strategy and tips
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-content">
    * Prioritize units **with mass attack skills** since they deal more damage.

    * Kill minions to deal DMG to BotE.

    * Attack as many times as possible, since it will help enemies stack up *\[Build Up]* and trigger :tooltip[Mass attack. Deals **50%** Reality DMG to all its allies and and additional DMG equal to **10%** Max HP.]{title="[Energy Explosion]"}.
      * Favor Follow-up attacks units.
  </Fragment>
