  <Fragment slot="stage-mechanism-title">
    ## Stage mechanism
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="stage-mechanism-content">
    * **Star** afflatus units gain **20%** stats increase in ATK, DEF and HP

    * The boss takes **75%** more **Genesis** DMG.

    * It self-cleanses all *\[Neg Status]* at end of turn.

    * If it didn't take damage in one turn, it will gain 2 layers of :effect{param="sturdiness"}.

    * Every **2** rounds, the boss will aim at **2** units with the highest HP percentages left and inflict **4** layers of :effect{code="FROSTBITE"}.

    * Its ultimate will inflict **5** stacks of :effect{code="FROSTBITE"} immediately, cause whole team to be :effect{param="freeze"}.

    * The tentacles will attack the unit with the highest HP percentage left at turn end, and give 1 stack of :effect{param="sturdiness"} to self and the boss. If at 4 moxie, they will cast Ultimate and inflict :effect{param="freeze"} on the target instead.

    * When a tentacle is killed by your attacks, the boss will take Genesis DMG equal to **15%** Max HP.

    * When DotA's Eureka is full, it activates a **shield phase**, where failing to destroy the shield within 3 rounds leads to the removal of all buffs from your team and a powerful AoE attack followed by :effect{param="freeze"}.

    #### Crisis Column mode

    * After casting their Ultimates, DotA and its Tentacles gain **a buff, which cannot be dispelled**, that reduces incoming damage.

    * **4** stacks of :effect{code="FROSTBITE"} will be applied per attack instead of **2**.
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-title">
    ## Strategy and tips
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-content">
    * Prioritize Genesis DMG & **Star** units since they deal more damage.

    * Avoid relying on debuff application due to DotA's end-round cleanse.

    * Attack the boss every turn to prevent it from gaining :effect{param="sturdiness"}.

    * :effect{code="FROSTBITE"} is a debuff that can be cleansed and immunity to it.

    * :effect{code="FROSTBITE"} stacks can be removed by taking active actions twice per turn.

    * When DotA in shield phase, focus on breaking its shield with all your attacks instead of trying to survive.

    #### Crisis Column mode

    * Preventing the execution of most ultimates is key by using character with :effect{param="silence"} or Moxie reduction skills.
  </Fragment>
