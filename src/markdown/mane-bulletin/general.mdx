# Mane's Bulletin

Mane's Bulletin is a raid event that makes you battle against a series of bosses, each offering unique mechanics and rewards. Engaging with this mode requires a developed roster, as you'll be using **4** characters to maximize damage output and sustain through the fight.

Rewards are based on the total damage inflicted.

To get all the major materials from this difficulty, you only need to hit S-rank

##### Scores
- **C** - 25,000
- **C+** - 50,000
- **B** - 120,000
- **B+** - 200,000
- **A** - 280,000
- **A+** - 360,000
- **S** - 450,000
- **SS** - 650,000
- **SSS** - 850,000
