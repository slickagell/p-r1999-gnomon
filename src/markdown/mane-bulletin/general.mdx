# Mane's Bulletin

Mane's Bulletin is a raid event that makes you battle against a series of bosses, each offering unique mechanics and rewards. You'll be using **4** characters to maximize damage output and sustain through the fight.

Rewards are based on the total damage inflicted.

##### Scores

* **C** - 25,000
* **C+** - 50,000
* **B** - 120,000
* **B+** - 200,000
* **A** - 280,000
* **A+** - 360,000
* **S** - 450,000
* **SS** - 650,000
* **SSS** - 850,000
