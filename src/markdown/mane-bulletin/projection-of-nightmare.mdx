    ## Stage mechanism
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="stage-mechanism-content">
    * The main boss is *Symbol of Nightmare* (humanoid)

    * There is other parts of the boss:
      * The *Root of Nightmare* (pyramid)
      * The *End of Nightmare* (left-arm)
      * The *Beginning of Nightmare* (right-arm)

    * At the end of each round, if our allies have 3 types of *\[Stats Up]*, *\[Pos Status]* or :effect{code="SHIELD"} buffs, gain Moxie **+1** and DMG Heal **+20%** for **2** rounds. If they have **5** or more buffs, also purify *\[Neg Status]* debuffs from themselves.

    * Allies after taking **2** actions in 1 turn will gain ATK **+30%** for **3** rounds. Taking **3** actions in 1 turn will increase DMG by **30%** for **3** rounds.

    * If any parts of the *Symbol* are destroyed by our allies, enter :effect{code="BEFOULED"} status.

    * If the *Root* is destroyed, deals DMG to the *Symbol* equal to its **30%** Max HP

    * At the beginning of new HP bar, it will inflict debuff that increase DMG Taken by **10%**.

    * It has a buff card that increases **10%** Reality and Mental DEF for **2** rounds.
      * If any of its arms is present, attack our allies and inflict **1** *\[Spite]* stack, then destroy both arms and resummon them.
      * If there's no arm left, resummon it immediately.

    * When *\[Spite]* reaches **6** stacks, it will turn into *\[Spite Entwined]* which prevents allies from casting **Buff/Debuff/Health/Counter** incantations.

    * Each arm will move twice per turn,
      * One uses 1-target attack skill and another uses AoE attack skill.
      * Each attack will also inflict **1** *\[Spite]* stack on 1 unit.
      * When being destroyed by our attacks, grants Moxie +1 to one of our allies with the lowest Moxie count.

    * The *Root* will grant **1** *\[Stability]* stack every turn for **4** turns. At the start of the round, if this unit has **4** Eureka, consume all Eureka and cleanse all *\[Stats Down]* from all allies. Its Ultimate will deal DMG to the unit with the highest ATK and inflict **2** *\[Spite]* stacks.

      #### Crisis Column mode

    * Enemies at the end of every **2** rounds will gain **1** :effect{code="STURDINESS"} stack.
  </Fragment>

  <Fragment slot="strategy-and-tips-title">
    ## Strategy and tips

    * Prioritize units with **Follow-up Attack** and **Buff** incantations.
    * Try to let units take multiple moves each turn to receive DMG buff
    * Use multiple Buff incantation to gain Moxie
    * Take down one of its arms to inflict :effect{code="BEFOULED"} stack to deal more DMG to the *Symbol*.
    * Take down the *Root* to deal massive DMG to the *Symbol*
  </Fragment>
