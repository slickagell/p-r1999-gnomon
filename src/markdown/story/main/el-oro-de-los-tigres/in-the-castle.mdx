**Sotheby**

* Hmm? What's that strange sound?

**Sotheby**

* Hmm ... Is the door shaking?

**Sotheby**

* No, something ... something is coming in!

*The lock shakes so violently, as if it were wrestling something invisible.*

**Sotheby**

*The air smells like fresh plant juice.*

**Sotheby**

* Wow ...

**Foundation Staff**

* Did you conjure these plants? Stop them now!

**Sotheby**

* Huh? What are you talking about?

**Sotheby**

* Oh my ...
* It's my ... Quick Quick Growth Potion for plants!

*Twigs thicken into branches, and more new branches come out from them. And new leaves, new sprouts. They crawl forward, forming a tree fortress around Sotheby.*

* I ...
