Acoustica artefact for **Vila**.

<br />

When Vila is deployed:

At the end of the round, all allies gain Eureka +1 and Moxie +1.

<br />

When Vila is deployed:

When entering battle, immediately enters the :effect{code="RUSALKA"} status permanently. At the start of the round, dispels 3 types of *\[Neg Status]*, *\[Stats Down]*, and or *\[Control]* effects from all allies.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Vila is a companion, have Vila enter the \[Rusalka\] status 0/1 time in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Vila is deployed:

When an ally attacks, deals additional DMG to the main target based on the rank of the incantation: a rank 1/2/3 incantation deals an additional **50%**/**120%**/**200%** DMG of the ally's corresponding damage type.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Vila is a companion, while in the \[Rusalka\] status, cast *\[Beating Heart\]* 0/5 times in total.]{className="text-enhance"}
