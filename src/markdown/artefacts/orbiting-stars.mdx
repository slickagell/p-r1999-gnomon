Acoustica artefact for **Kaalaa Baunaa**.

<br/> 

When Kaalaa Baunaa is deployed:

When a :effect{code="PLANET"} is removed, grant 1 of the following effects to all allies based on the type of planet removed: :effect{code="DUSK_SATURN_DIVINATION"}, :effect{code="DUSK_MARS_DIVINATION"}, or :effect{code="DUSK_FULL_MOON_DIVINATION"}.

<br/> 

When Kaalaa Baunaa is deployed:

After casting an ultimate, gains 1 effect based on the :effect{code="PLANET"} that had the highest number removed (each effect can trigger only once per round).

When removing 1/2/3+ :effect{code="SATURN"}: makes another ally immediately cast a rank 1/2/3 Incantation #1.

When removing 1/2/3+ :effect{code="MARS"}: makes another ally immediately cast a rank 1/2/3 Incantation #2.

When removing 1/2+ :effect{code="FULL_MOON"}: makes another ally immediately cast their ultimate 1/2 times. 

:text[Enhance requirements: When Kaalaa Baunaa is a companion, have Kaalaa Baunaa cast ultimates 0/2 times in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br/> 

When Kaalaa Baunaa is a companion:

When an ally performs an extra action, the ally gets DMG Dealt **+50%**.

Obtains 2 random Epic Extra Action artefacts (triggers only 1 time). 

:text[Enhance requirements: When Kaalaa Baunaa is a companion, have Kaalaa Baunaa cast ultimates 0/5 times in total.]{className="text-enhance"}
