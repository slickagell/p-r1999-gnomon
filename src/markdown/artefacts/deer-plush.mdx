Acoustica artefact for **Jessica**.

<br/> 

When Jessica is deployed:

After this unit casts and attacks with a basic incantation, inflicts 3 instances of :effect{code="POISON"} on the target for 3 rounds.

<br/> 

When Jessica is deployed:

After entering battle and at the start of every 2 rounds thereafter, grants a Shield with (Jessica's ATK x**300%**) HP for 2 rounds. When an ally with this Shield is attacked, inflicts 1 stack of :effect{code="HAG_S_BANE"} on the attacker for 2 rounds, and grants ATK **+5%** to Jessica. 

:text[Enhance requirements: When Jessica is a companion, have Jessica actively cast incantations 0/5 times in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br/> 

When Jessica is deployed:

After casting an ultimate, inflicts :effect{code="PETRIFY"} on the enemy for 1 round; then consumes up to 4 instances of :effect{code="POISON"} and resolves :effect{code="HAG_S_BANE"} on the target 1 time for each stack consumed (this effect does not decrease :effect{code="HAG_S_BANE"}'s round duration); allies attacking enemies in :effect{code="PETRIFY"} will not remove the :effect{code="PETRIFY"} status. 

:text[Enhance requirements: When Jessica is a companion, have Jessica actively cast incantations 0/10 times in total.]{className="text-enhance"}
