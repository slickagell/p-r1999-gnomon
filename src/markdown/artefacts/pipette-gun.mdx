Acoustica artefact for **Medicine Pocket**.

<br />

When Medicine Pocket is a companion:

Gains 1 random Epic Craft artefact (triggers up to 1 time).

<br />

When Medicine Pocket is deployed:

When entering battle, for every 1 upgraded Craft artefact in the layout, all allies gain 4 effects from :effect{code="COLLECTION_OF_PERMANENT_BUFFS"}.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Medicine Pocket is a companion, obtain 0/2 Craft artefacts in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When entering battle, for every 1 Medication in the layout, places 1 special incantation *\[Strong Painkiller]* into the Spellbox (triggers up to 5 times).

:text[Enhance requirements: When Medicine Pocket is a companion, obtain 0/5 Medications in total.]{className="text-enhance"}
