Acoustica artefact for **Voyager**.

When Voyager is deployed: At the end of the round, deals (ATK x**500%**) Genesis DMG to all enemies, if any target has 3 or less Moxie, then this attack deals an additional **500%** Genesis DMG, and inflicts Moxie -1 on a random enemy.

<br />

When Voyager is a companion: At the end of every 2 rounds, inflicts :effect{code="DISCONCERT"} on all enemies for 1 round.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Voyager is a companion, complete a Rest Site 0/2 times.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Voyager is a companion: When an ally with :effect{code="INTERSTELLAR_BLESSING"} is being attacked, the attacker loses 1 Moxie, and the ally recovers (Missing HP x**50%**) HP.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Voyager is a companion, enter Old Town.]{className="text-enhance"}
