Acoustica artefact for **Charlie**.

When Charlie is deployed: At the start of the round, when HP is above **40%**, enters the :effect{code="TYRANT_KING"} status for 1 round; when HP is below **60%**, enters the :effect{code="SAGE_KING"} status for 1 round.

<br />

When Charlie is deployed: When under the :effect{code="SAGE_KING"} status, DMG Taken **-200%**.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Charlie is a companion, deploy 0/2 times.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Charlie is deployed: When under the :effect{code="TYRANT_KING"} status, at the start of each round, generates 1 rank 3 *\[Thunder to Thee]* and 1 rank 3 *\[Tempest to Thee]*.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Charlie is a companion, deploy 0/5 times.]{className="text-enhance"}
