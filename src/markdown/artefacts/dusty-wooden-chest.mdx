Whenever entering a Safe Zone, this artefact can be opened using a Key of the Dead.

There's a **50%** chance to gain a Common artefact, a **30%** chance to gain a Rare artefact, or a **20%** chance to gain an Epic artefact.

This artefact removes itself after being opened.
