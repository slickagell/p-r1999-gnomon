Acoustica artefact for **Druvis III**.

When Druvis III is a companion: Whenever entering a Discovery 3 times, gains 3 Mistletoe Twigs.

<br />

When Druvis III is deployed: At the start of every 2 rounds, for every 1 Mistletoe Twigs in the layout, places 1 special incantation *\[Ripe Mistletoe Berries]* into the Spellbox.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Druvis III is a companion, obtain 0/1 Mistletoe Twigs artefact.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Druvis III is deployed: At the start of the round, for every 1 Curio in the layout, gains a rank 3 precast incantation *\[Wind into the Woods]* (triggers up to 3 times per round).

:text[Enhance requirements: When Druvis III is a companion, complete 0/3 battles with Mistletoe Twigs in the layout.]{className="text-enhance"}
