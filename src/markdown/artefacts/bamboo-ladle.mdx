Acoustica artefact for **Jiu Niangzi**.

<br/> 

When Jiu Niangzi is deployed:

Gains Assembled Goldberg Brewer (triggers up to 1 time).

At the start of the round, for every 1 Medication in the layout, gains 3 stacks of *[Liquor]*.

<br/> 

When Jiu Niangzi is a companion:

Whenever leaving a Safe Zone, for every 1 Medication in the layout and stash, gains 20 Goldfinch Coins. 

:text[Enhance requirements: When Jiu Niangzi is a companion, complete 0/1 battle with 5 or more Medications in the layout and stash.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br/> 

When Jiu Niangzi is deployed:

After casting a special incantation, casts a rank 3 *[Bottoms Up]* on the enemy with the lowest HP percentage. 

:text[Enhance requirements: When Jiu Niangzi is a companion, obtain 0/8 Medications in total.]{className="text-enhance"}
