When an ally gets an increase in Stress, that ally has a **20%** chance to recover (own Max HP x**2%**) HP.

When entering a Safe Zone, devour all Common artefacts in the stash.

For every 5 artefacts devoured, trigger an additional instance of the recovery effect.

(Artefacts Devoured: 0).
