Acoustica artefact for **Lorelei**.

When Lorelei is deployed: At the start of the round, draw 10 extra incantations.

When an ally attacks, the attack gains bonuses based on the incantations present in the Spelldock (The Spelldock is also known as the "incantation area,” or your "hand"):
- Each attack incantation grants an additional Reality DMG **+20%** / Mental DMG **+20%**.
- Each buff incantation grants Critical Rate **+10%**.
- Each debuff incantation grants Penetration Rate **+20%**.
- Each special incantation grants ATK **+10%**.
<br/>
- When Lorelei is deployed: At the start of the round, deletes all Jinxed Incantation from the Spellbox (triggers up to 1 time), and inserts 1 [Moonstone Powder] and 1 [Belladonna Juice].

:text[Enhance requirements: When Lorelei is a companion, upgrade Catalyst to Lv.5+.]{className="text-enhance"}
<br/>
- When Lorelei is a companion: Whenever completing a Present Danger/Impending Crisis, recovers 100 Stimulus.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Lorelei is a companion, upgrade Catalyst to Lv.7+.]{className="text-enhance"}
