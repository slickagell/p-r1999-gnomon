Acoustica artefact for **Willow**.

When Willow is deployed: When Black Potion, Cantarella Toxin, or Cantarella Viscotoxin is in the layout, at the end of the round, inflicts 2 instances of :effect{code="POISON"} on all enemies for 2 rounds.

<br />

When Willow is deployed: When there are at least 3 Black Potion, Cantarella Toxin, and Cantarella Viscotoxin in the layout combined, gain Genesis DMG Dealt **+100%** for self.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Willow is a companion, deploy 0/2 times.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Willow is deployed: When there are at least 5 Black Potions, Cantarella Toxins, or Cantarella Viscotoxins in the layout combined, at the end of the round, inflicts 1 stack of :effect{code="HAGS_BANE"} on all enemies.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Willow is a companion, upgrade Catalyst to Lv.7+.]{className="text-enhance"}
