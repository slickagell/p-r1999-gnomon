Acoustica artefact for **Fatutu**.

<br/> 

When Fatutu is deployed:

When entering battle, gains an additional 20 stacks of :effect{code="SPIRIT_SHELL_SELF"} for self. At the start of the round, deploys all of her :effect{code="SPIRIT_SHELL"} stacks onto a random enemy.

<br/> 

When Fatutu is deployed:

After any unit takes an action, if any ally is under a *[Control]* status, removes 5 stacks of :effect{code="SHARED_SHELLS_SHARED_FATE"} from all allies, then dispels *[Control]* statuses held on allies (triggers up to 3 times per battle). 

:text[Enhance requirements: When Fatutu is a companion, complete 0/3 path nodes.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br/> 

When Fatutu is deployed:

At the end of the round, casts a 1 rank *[Ocean in the Shell]*. 

:text[Enhance requirements: When Fatutu is a companion, complete 0/5 path nodes.]{className="text-enhance"}
