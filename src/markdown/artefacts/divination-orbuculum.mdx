Acoustica artefact for Matilda.

When Sonetto is aiding Matilda: Gains ATK **+100%**, Critical Rate **+100%**, Critical DMG **+200%**.

<br />

When Sonetto is aiding Matilda: After casting an ultimate, gains Critical Rate **+100%** and Critical DMG **+100%** for 1 round (stackable), then Sonetto casts an ultimate 1 time.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Matilda is a companion, upgrade Catalyst to Lv.5+.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Sonetto is aiding Matilda: Excess Critical Rate is converted into Critical DMG. At the end of the round, gains 2 stacks of *\[Anticipation - Precast]* and 2 stacks of *\[Anticipation - Support]*.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Matilda is a companion, upgrade Catalyst to Lv.7+.]{className="text-enhance"}
