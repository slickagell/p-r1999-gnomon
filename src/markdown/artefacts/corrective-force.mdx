Acoustica artefact for **Tooth Fairy**.

<br />

When Tooth Fairy is deployed:

When entering battle, for each Medication in the layout, all allies gain Critical Rate **+10%**, and Tooth Fairy gains ATK **+10%**.

When obtaining Fairy's Blessing, obtain an additional 3 Medications.

<br />

When Tooth Fairy is deployed: At the start of the round, Tooth Fairy gains 3 stacks of :effect{code="BABY_TOOTH"}. When :effect{code="BABY_TOOTH"}'s Stats Down effect is triggered, dispels 2 random *\[Stats Down]*, *\[Neg Status]*, or *\[Control]* effects from allies, then all allies gain 1 stack of :effect{code="INCORPOREAL"}.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Tooth Fairy is a companion, obtain 0/4 Medications in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Tooth Fairy is deployed:

When :effect{code="BABY_TOOTH"}'s Stats Down effect is triggered, allied excess Critical Rate is converted into Critical DMG for 1 round.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Tooth Fairy is a companion, obtain 0/8 Medications in total.]{className="text-enhance"}
