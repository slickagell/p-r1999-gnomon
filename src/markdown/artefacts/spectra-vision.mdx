Acoustica artefact for **An-an Lee**.

<br />

When An-an Lee is deployed:

At the start of the round, draws 3 incantations from the Spellbox and grants incantation rank +1 to all allies.

After casting a rank 3 incantation, casts an ultimate 1 time.

<br />

When An-an Lee is deployed:

When casting an ultimate, for each rank 1/2/3 incantation in the AP Area, deals an additional **25%**/**100%**/**250%** Mental DMG.

:text[Enhance requirements: When An-an Lee is a companion, complete a commission 0/1 time and get rewarded.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When An-an Lee is deployed:

After casting an ultimate, inflicts :effect{code="DAZE"} on all enemies for 1 round.

:text[Enhance requirements: When An-an Lee is a companion, complete a commission 0/2 times and get rewarded.]{className="text-enhance"}
