Acoustica artefact for **Yenisei**.

<br />

When Yenisei is deployed:

When entering battle, gains Max HP **+50%**. The :effect{param="immunity"} status granted by the ultimate lasts 1 additional round.

<br />

When Yenisei is deployed:

At the start of the round, gains 1 stack of :effect{param="flow"}. At the end of the round, if :effect{param="flow"} has reached 3 stacks, consumes 1 stack of :effect{param="flow"} to recover (own Max HP x**15%**) HP for all allies, then gains Moxie +1 for self.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Yenisei is a companion, have all companions cast rank 2/3 incantations 0/15 times in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Yenisei is a deployed:

At the end of the round, if the total ranks of incantations played this round reach 3/5/7, Yenisei and the adjacent ally behind gain 1 stack of :effect{code="ANTICIPATION_PRECAST"}/:effect{code="ANTICIPATION_PRECAST_II"}/:effect{code="ANTICIPATION_PRECAST_III"}.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Yenisei is a companion, have all companions cast rank 2/3 incantations 0/30 times in total.]{className="text-enhance"}
