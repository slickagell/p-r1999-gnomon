Acoustica artefact for **Kakania**.

<br />

When Kakania is a companion:

Whenever completing a path node, all companions recover (Max HP x**30%**) HP and get Stress -10.

<br />

When Kakania is deployed:

When entering battle, grant Max HP **+20%** to all allies.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Kakania is a companion, complete a Rest Site 0/1 time.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Kakania is a companion:

Obtains 1 Everheart (triggers only 1 time). Upgrades all Everhearts in the layout, increasing the Genesis DMG Dealt gained by allies to **200%**.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Kakania is a companion, complete a Rest Site 0/2 times.]{className="text-enhance"}
