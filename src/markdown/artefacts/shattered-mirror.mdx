Acoustica artefact for **Semmelweis**.

<br />

When Semmelweis is a companion:

When entering a Safe Zone, obtains 1 Spirit Decanter.

When Semmelweis is deployed:

When an ally takes an action, all allies lose (Current HP x**1%**) HP and gain 20 stacks of :effect{code="VERVE"}.

<br />

When Semmelweis is a companion:

Obtains 8 random Drives (triggers up to 1 time).

When Semmelweis is deployed:

After casting an ultimate, all allies gain 2 stacks of :effect{code="MORIBUND"} and Moxie +2.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Semmelweis is a companion, obtain 0/4 Disks in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Semmelweis is deployed:

For every 2 Disks in the layout, gains 1 stack of :effect{code="MORIBUND"} and 3 stacks of :effect{code="LUCKY"} for self.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Semmelweis is a companion, complete the "Gruß an Aachen" event and gain the corresponding artefact.]{className="text-enhance"}
