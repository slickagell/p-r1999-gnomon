Acoustica artefact for **Mercuria**.

<br />

When Mercuria is a companion:

Whenever entering a *\[Into the Unknown]*, gain an additional 100 Gold. Whenever entering a *\[Discovery]*, obtain an additional 1 random artefact.

<br />

When Mercuria is deployed:

After Mercuria casts an ultimate, all allies gain :effect{param="empower-incantation-ii"} for 3 rounds.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Mercuria is a companion, have all companions cast rank 3 incantations 0/10 times in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Mercuria is deployed:

When casting an ultimate, for each stack of :effect{param="cosmic-energy"} held on self, deals an additional **30%** Mental DMG.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Mercuria is a companion, have all companions cast rank 3 incantations 0/20 times in total.]{className="text-enhance"}
