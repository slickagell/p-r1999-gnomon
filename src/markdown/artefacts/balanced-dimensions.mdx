Acoustica artefact for **6**.

<br />

When 6 is deployed:

At the start of the round, inflicts 2 effects from :effect{code="COLLECTION_OF_DEBUFFS"} on all enemies. At the end of the round, for every 2 Eureka consumed from self in the round, gain Moxie +1 for self. At the start of the round, gain Eureka +2 for self.

<br />

When 6 is deployed:

Right before casting an ultimate, recovers all Eureka. When attacking, for each Eureka held on self, this attack gains an additional DMG Dealt **+15%**.

:text[Enhance requirements: When 6 is a companion, have 6 spend/gain 0/5 Eureka in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When 6 is deployed:

Ultimate Might **+100%**. For every 5 Eureka consumed from self within a round, recover (6's ATK x**666%**) HP for all allies.

:text[Enhance requirements: When 6 is a companion, have 6 spend/gain 0/10 Eureka in total.]{className="text-enhance"}
