:text[Enhance requirements: When TTT is a companion, obtain 0/3 Voltaic artefacts in total.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

:text[Enhance requirements: When TTT is a companion, complete 0/1 battle with 5 or more activated Voltaic artefacts in the layout.]{className="text-enhance"}
