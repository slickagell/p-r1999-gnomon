Acoustica artefact for **Necrologist**.

<br />

When Necrologist is a companion:

After any ally becomes Lost, revive the ally immediately and recover **50%** of the ally's Max HP (starts with 1 use, gains an additional use when entering a Safe Zone).

<br />

When Necrologist is aiding:

When entering battle, all allies gain 3 stacks of :effect{param="moribund"}; whenever :effect{param="moribund"} disappears from an ally, dispel negative statuses from all allies and recover (their Max HP x**50%**) HP for all allies (triggers up to 2 times per battle).

:text[Enhance requirements: When Necrologist is a companion, complete an Impending Crisis 0/1 time.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Necrologist is aiding:

Whenever an ally is dying, all allies gain DMG dealt + **50%**, stackable.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Necrologist is a companion, complete an Impending Crisis 0/2 times.]{className="text-enhance"}
