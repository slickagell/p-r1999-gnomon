Acoustica artefact for **Anjo Nala**.

When Anjo Nala is deployed: At the start of an allied round, if this unit is below **50%** HP, consumes 5 Stimulus to recover **100%** of own Max HP and gains 1 stack of :effect{code="MORIBUND"}. Whenever an ally defeats an enemy, recovers 5 Stimulus.

<br />

When Anjo Nala is a companion: Whenever entering an Impending Crisis, gains Max Stimulus +10 and recovers Stimulus to full.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Anjo Nala is a companion, complete an Impending Crisis 0/1 time.]{className="text-enhance"}

<br />

When Anjo Nala is deployed: After an ally actively casts an incantation, this unit and the bound ally gain 15 stacks of :effect{code="CEREMONIOUS"}.

:text[Enhance requirements: When Anjo Nala is a companion, complete an Impending Crisis 0/2 times.]{className="text-enhance"}
