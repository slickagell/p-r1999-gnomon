---
title: "Mistletoe Berries"
code: "MISTLETOE_BERRIES"
type: "GENERAL"
isSpecific: "mode"
---

Grants 2 stacks of :effect{param="unsparing"}, :effect{param="lucky"}, :effect{param="schemed"}, and :effect{param="glean"} to an ally. If the target already has one of these buffs, double the stacks granted correspondingly.
