title: "Mistletoe Berries"
code: "MISTLETOE_BERRIES"
type: "GENERAL"

---

Grants 2 stacks of :effect{code="UNSPARING"}, :effect{code="LUCKY"}, :effect{code="SCHEMED"}, and :effect{code="GLEAN"} to an ally. If the target already has one of these buffs, double the stacks granted correspondingly.
