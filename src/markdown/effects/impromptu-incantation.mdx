---
title: "Impromptu Incantation"
code: "IMPROMPTU_INCANTATION"
type: "GENERAL"
---

1-target attack. Deals **200%** Mental DMG to a random enemy, and this attack deals an additional X% Mental DMG, where X is equal to (the total points of Inspiration granted to Impromptu Incantation x 15); if Impromptu Incantation has at least 3/6/12/20 points of Inspiration, Impromptu Incantation gains attack instances +1/2/3/5 (Impromptu Incantation's damage calculation is based on the average stats of all allies).
