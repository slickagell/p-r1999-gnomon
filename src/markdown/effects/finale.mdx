At the start of the round, grants 3 stacks of :effect{code="PREIGNITION"} and 1 stack of :effect{code="POWER_BURST"} to all allies. When the highest enemy :effect{code="BURN"} stack reaches 6 or higher, casts :effect{code="INTERMEZZO"}, and gain Incantation Might **+15%** for this attack (undispellable).
