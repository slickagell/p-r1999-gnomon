At the start of the round, grants 3 stacks of :effect{param="preignition"} and 1 stack of :effect{param="power-burst"} to all allies. When the highest enemy :effect{param="burn"} stack reaches 6 or higher, casts :effect{param="intermezzo"}, and gain Incantation Might **+15%** for this attack (undispellable).
