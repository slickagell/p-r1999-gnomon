At the start of the round, recovers all Eureka, converts all own incantations into :effect{param="regards-from-the-sky-id-0"}, and casts them all if able.

At the end of the round, casts 1 :effect{param="regards-from-the-sky-id-0"}.

When the channel status ends, grants Moxie +1/2/3 to self based on the current Gust Force Field.

Cannot gain Moxie during channeling.
