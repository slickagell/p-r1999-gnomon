title: "Hyperthymesia"
code: "HYPERTHYMESIA_ID_1"

This status channels until the next round, ending right before the AP Area resolves. When this channel status ends, the memorized incantations are added to the spell queue and casted alongside all other incantations from the round. These memorized incantations are considered actively cast, but do not grant any Moxie. Afterward, grant attack instances +2 to *\[Impromptu Incantation]* for the round, and all non-final attack instances of Impromptu Incantation this round inflict the :effect{param="interpretation-id-1"} status on the attack target, with the final attack instance also dealing an additional (the target's stacks of *\[Interpretation]* x **80**)% Mental DMG to each enemy target holding the *\[Interpretation]* status. Aleph cannot act while channeling, and all stacks of *\[Interpretation]* are removed from enemies after the final attack instance of Impromptu Incantation.
