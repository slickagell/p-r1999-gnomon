---
title: "Hag's Bane"
code: "HAGS_BANE"
type: "NEG_STATUS"
isSpecific: "character"
---

Counts as a type of :effect{code="POISON"}: at the end of the round, takes (the user's ATK x**20%**) Genesis DMG, lasts 2 rounds. After each resolve of *\[Hag's Bane]*, the target takes a compounding **40%** more damage from each subsequent resolve of Hag's Bane for the rest of the round, this compounding effect can stack up to 4 times, and this status effect stacks up to 10 times.
