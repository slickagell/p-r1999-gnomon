---
title: "Inspiration"
code: "INSPIRATION"
type: "GENERAL"
---

For every 1 Eureka consumed, accumulate 1 point of *\[Inspiration]*.

At the start of each round, randomly distribute Inspiration points onto basic incantations (each incantation may receive up to 5 points of Inspiration, and at the end of the round, the points of Inspiration on unused incantations are cleared).

When actively casting an incantation with *\[Inspiration]* points on it, add an :effect{param="impromptu-incantation"} to the end of the spell queue, and all Inspiration points on these casted incantations will be granted to the :effect{param="impromptu-incantation"}.
