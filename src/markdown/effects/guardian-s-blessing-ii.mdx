DMG Dealt **+25%**, DMG Taken **-25%**. When attacking/being attacked/at the end of the round, Liang Yue casts a rank 2 :effect{param="banish-evil"} on the target/the attacker/the enemy with the lowest HP. After a successful cast, the remaining duration gets deducted by 1 round; otherwise, no duration is deducted.
