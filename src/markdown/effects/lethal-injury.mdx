---
title: "Lethal Injury"
code: "LETHAL_INJURY"
type: "GENERAL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

When being attacked, trigger :effect{param="assassination"} and remove 1 stack. When taking an *\[Assassination]* attack, for every 100 points of Critical Technique the attacker has more than the target, this attack gains Final DMG Dealt **+1%**; this effect does not remove stacks.
