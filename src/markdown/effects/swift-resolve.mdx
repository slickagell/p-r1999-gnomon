*\[Bane of All Evil]* requires 1 less *\[Justice Talon]*/*\[Peace Talon]*, and deals an additional **20%** Reality DMG (stacks up to 2 times, all stacks are removed after trigger).

*\[Bane of All Evil - Balance]* deals an additional **40%** Reality DMG (stackable, stacks of *\[Swift Resolve]* are not removed after trigger).
