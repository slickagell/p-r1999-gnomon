Force Field effect: This force field benefits all allies, and the added Reality DMG is considered dealt by Flutterpage herself.

<br />

When an ally attacks:

Add an additional **18%** Reality DMG to the attack, with an additional **18%** Reality DMG to the main target.

<br />

When an ally performs an extra action:

Consume 2 of Flutterpage's Eureka and add an additional **18%** Reality DMG to the attack, with an additional **18%** Reality DMG to the main target.
For each stack of *\[Gust]* Flutterpage has, grant DMG Dealt **+2.5%** and Genesis DMG **+2.5%** to the attack.
