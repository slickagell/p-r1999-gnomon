code: "SONG_OF_PASSION"

Critical Rate **+20%** and Critical DMG **+20%**. Mental DMG characters gain an additional Critical Rate **+10%** and Critical DMG **+20%**; when attacking with a rank 1/2/3 incantation, deal an additional **50%/120%/200%** DMG of the holder's damage type (undispellable).
