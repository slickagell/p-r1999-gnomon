---
title: "A Dream That Never Grows Old"
code: "A_DREAM_THAT_NEVER_GROWS_OLD"
type: "CHANNEL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

At the start of the round, recovers all Eureka, converts all own incantations into :effect{code="REGARDS_FROM_THE_SKY"}, and casts them all if able.

At the end of the round, casts 1 :effect{code="REGARDS_FROM_THE_SKY"}. 

When the channel status ends, grants Moxie +1/2/3 to self based on the current Gust Force Field. Cannot gain Moxie during channeling.
