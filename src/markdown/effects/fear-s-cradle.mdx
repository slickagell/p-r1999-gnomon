---
title: "Fear's Cradle"
code: "FEAR_S_CRADLE"
type: "POS_STATUS"
isSpecific: "character"
---

All allies can score critical hits with their :effect{param="poison"} effects. When an ally takes an action, every *\[Stats Down]*, *\[Neg Status]*, or *\[Control]* status the ally inflicts on an enemy target through this action has a **50%** chance to inflict 1 instance of :effect{param="poison"} on that target for 1 round: at the end of the round, deals (the user's ATK x**20%**) Genesis DMG to the target (undispellable).
