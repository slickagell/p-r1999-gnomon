---
title: "Flood of Fiction"
code: "FLOOD_OF_FICTION"
type: "GENERAL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

Make the ally with the highest Moxie consume all their current Moxie to cast an ultimate, then recover the consumed Moxie.

(This ultimate does not count as an extra action, and the recovered Moxie cannot exceed the Moxie required to cast the ultimate).
