At the start of the round, upgrades based on *\[Dynamo]* transferred: 50+ upgrades to :effect{param="pulsing-field-lv-2"}, 120+ upgrades to :effect{param="pulsing-field-lv-3"}. When removed, transferred *\[Dynamo]* is also cleared.
