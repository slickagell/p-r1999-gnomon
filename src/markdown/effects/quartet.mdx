Gain ATK +300; at the start of each round, gain an additional (the accumulated points of Inspiration x 5) ATK; this effect is recalculated at the start of each round, and for each ally in the *\[Quartet]* status, Barcarola gains an additional **15%** bonus for this ATK increase effect. After casting an attack-type incantation, grant 1 additional point of Inspiration to :effect{code="IMPROMPTU_INCANTATION"}, and consume 1 Eureka from each character in the Quartet status (undispellable).
