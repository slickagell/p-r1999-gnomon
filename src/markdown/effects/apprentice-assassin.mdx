title: "Apprentice Assassin"

After getting to 8 stacks, gains :effect{param="master-assassin"}. If there are other *\[Assassination]* characters on the team, grants :effect{param="master-assassin"} to other allies (**70%** of effect for *\[Assassination]* characters and **50%** for others).
