---
title: "Additional Genesis DMG"
code: "ADDITIONAL_GENESIS_DMG"
type: "GENERAL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

When attacking an enemy in the *\[Poison]* status with basic incantation/Ultimate, deal an additional (rank 1 :effect{param="work-in-the-dark-id-1"}/:effect{param="death-be-not-unkind"}'s Reality DMG x**50%/50%**) Genesis DMG.

For every 1 instance of *\[Poison]* on the target, deal an additional (rank 1 :effect{param="work-in-the-dark-id-1"}/:effect{param="death-be-not-unkind"}'s Reality DMG x**1%/1%**) Genesis DMG.

When there are 10 or more instances of *\[Poison]* on the target: increase the Genesis DMG ratio to **2%/2%**.

When there are 20 or more instances of *\[Poison]* on the target: the Genesis DMG can score a critical hit.
