---
title: "Concealed Assassination"
code: "CONCEALED_ASSASSINATION"
type: "CHANNEL"
isSpecific: "monster"
---

If this channel status is still active at the start of the round, deals **300%** Reality DMG to the enemy with the lowest HP percentage and inflicts :effect{code="DAZE"} on the target for 1 round (cannot act while channeling).
