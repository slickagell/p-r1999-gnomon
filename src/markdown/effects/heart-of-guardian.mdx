---
title: "Heart of Guardian"
code: "HEART_OF_GUARDIAN"
type: "CHANNEL"
isSpecific: "monster"
---

When this Channel status is active at the start of the round, cast a self buff incantation that grants a *\[Shield]* with (own Max HP x**20%**) HP to self for 2 rounds (when channeling, DMG Taken **+25%**, cannot act. This incantation is not an extra action and is removed after being attacked by an Ultimate).
