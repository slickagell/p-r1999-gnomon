---
title: "Bane of All Evil"
code: "BANE_OF_ALL_EVIL"
type: "GENERAL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

When reaching 6 *\[Justice Talon]* and or *\[Peace Talon]* combined, consume 6 of these *\[Justice Talon]* and of *\[Peace Talon]* to cast a *\[Bane of All Evil]* based on the balance of Justice Talons to Peace Talons:

Cast :effect{param="bane-of-all-evil-justice"} when there's more *\[Justice Talon]*.

Cast :effect{param="bane-of-all-evil-peace"} when there's more *\[Peace Talon]*.

Cast :effect{param="bane-of-all-evil-balance"} when there's an equal number of talons.
