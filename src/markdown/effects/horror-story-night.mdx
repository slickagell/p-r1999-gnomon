:effect{code="ARRAY"} effect: :effect{code="POISON"} effects on enemies do not decrease in duration on resolve. At the start of the enemy's round, resolve 1 round of their :effect{code="POISON"} effects. After the array ends or is replaced, immediately resolve :effect{code="POISON"} on all enemies.
