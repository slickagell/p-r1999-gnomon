:effect{param="array"} effect: :effect{param="poison"} effects on enemies do not decrease in duration on resolve. At the start of the enemy's round, resolve 1 round of their :effect{param="poison"} effects. After the array ends or is replaced, immediately resolve :effect{param="poison"} on all enemies.
