Mass attack. Deals **100%** Reality DMG to all enemies. Consume 10 stacks of *[Dynamo]* to grant Penetration Rate **+50%** to this attack. If reinforced, this attack deals an **50%** additional Reality DMG to targets below **50%** HP, ignores Dodge and does not trigger Reflect or Riposte.
