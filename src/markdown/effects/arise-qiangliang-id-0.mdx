---
title: "Arise, Qiangliang!"
code: "ARISE_QIANGLIANG_ID_0"
type: "SPECIAL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

Enhances and categorizes the allied Spelldock into different sections, granting bonuses to incantations based on the section the incantations are played from:

The 3 rightmost incantations played from the Spelldock gain rank +1 before resolve;

The 3 leftmost incantations played from the Spelldock gain +1 round duration to the :tooltip[*\[Stats Up\]*, *\[Stats Down\]*, *\[Counter\]*, *\[Pos Status\]*, *\[Neg Status\]*]{title="[Specified Selection]"} effects that the incantations actively apply.

The incantations played from the overlap section of the Spelldock gain both of the bonus effects above.
