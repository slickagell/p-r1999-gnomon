title: "Hag's Bane"
code: "HAG_S_BANE_ID_2"
type: "NEG_STATUS"
isSpecific: "character"
---

Counts as a type of *\[Poison]*: at the end of the round, takes (the user's ATK x**24%**) Genesis DMG, lasts 2 rounds. After each resolve of *\[Hag's Bane]*, the target takes a compounding **45%** more damage from each subsequent resolve of Hag's Bane for the rest of the round, this compounding effect can stack up to 4 times, and this status effect stacks up to 10 times.
