---
title: "Fate Amidst the Fog"
code: "FATE_AMIDST_THE_FOG"
type: "SPECIAL"
isSpecific: "mode"
---

At the end of the round, if HP is above **50%**, lose **15%** of current HP and gain a Shield with (Missing HP x**200%**) HP; if HP is below **50%**, gain a Shield with (own ATK x**100%**) HP; these Shields last 1 round and grant DMG Taken **-20%**.
