Deals **400%** Reality DMG to all enemies. Excess Critical Rate is converted into Critical DMG. Considered as a rank 2 incantation.

For every 1 *\[Justice Talon]* this attack consumes, this attack gains Critical Rate **+5%**, Critical DMG **+20%**, and DMG Dealt **+10%**.

For every 1 *\[Justice Talon]* or *\[Peace Talon]* this attack consumes, grant 1 stack of :effect{param="guardian-s-resolve-id-0"} to all allies for 2 rounds.
