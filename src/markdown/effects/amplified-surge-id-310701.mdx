Reaching :effect{param="pulsing-field-lv-3"} immediately grants Moxie +3 and 5 stacks of *\[Decryption Key]* to *\[Structure Analysis]*, *\[Frequency Analysis]*, and *\[Hyper Analysis]*.
Under :effect{param="pulsing-field-lv-3"}, after entering *\[Structure Analysis]*/*\[Frequency Analysis]*/*\[Hyper Analysis]* status, the corresponding Analysis immediately gains 5 stacks of *\[Decryption Key]*, and for every stack of *\[Decryption Key]* to *\[Hyper Analysis]*, Reality DMG **+100%**.
