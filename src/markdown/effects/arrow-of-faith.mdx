---
title: "Arrow of Faith"
code: "ARROW_OF_FAITH"
type: "GENERAL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

1-target attack. Deal **100%** Reality DMG; if the target's HP is below **50%**, deal an additional **50%** Reality DMG. This attack counts as an *[Assassination]*, ignores Dodge, and cannot trigger DMG sharing or other allies' additional DMG.
