---
title: "Blood Sacrifice"
code: "BLOOD_SACRIFICE"
type: "GENERAL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

At the end of the round, consumes as much *\[Bloodtithe]* as possible; for every 8 points of *\[Bloodtithe]* consumed, gains 3 points of *\[Blood Sacrifice]*, up to 24 points of *\[Blood Sacrifice]*. If carrying at least 3 points of *\[Blood Sacrifice]*, casts *\[A Thousand Scars]*; otherwise, casts *\[A Thousand Springs]* instead. Cannot act while channeling.
