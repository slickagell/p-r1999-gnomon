---
title: "Serpentine Scales"
code: "SERPENTINE_SCALES"
type: "SPECIAL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

At the end of the round, take ((**50%** + (number of stacks x 20)%) x Hissabeth's ATK) Genesis DMG. This effect can critically hit and benefits from Ultimate Might bonuses. After each trigger of this effect, 1 stack of Serpentine Scales is converted into 1 stack of :effect{param="snake-s-den"} and granted to Hissabeth. When the holder of this status dies, the remaining stacks of *\[Serpentine Scales]* are transferred onto the ally with the highest ATK. This status can stack up to 8 times and is undispellable.
