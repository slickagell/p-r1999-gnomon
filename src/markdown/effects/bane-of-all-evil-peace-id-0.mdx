code: "BANE_OF_ALL_EVIL_PEACE"
type: "GENERAL"
isSpecific: "character"

Deals **400%** Reality DMG to all enemies.

For every 1 *\[Peace Talon]* this attack consumes, right before the attack, inflict 1 instance of :effect{param="poison"} for 1 round on the target and 3 stack of :effect{param="burn"} on the target.

For every instance of :effect{param="poison"} on the target, this attack gains DMG Dealt **+10%**. For every stack of :effect{param="burn"} on the target, this attack gains Penetration Rate **+3%**.

For every *\[Justice Talon]* or *\[Peace Talon]* this attack consumes, grants 1 stack of :effect{param="guardian-s-resolve-id-0"} to all allies for 2 rounds.
