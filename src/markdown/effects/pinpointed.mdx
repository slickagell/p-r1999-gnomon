---
title: "Pinpointed"
code: "PINPOINTED"
type: "NEG_STATUS"
isSpecific: "character"
---

DMG Taken Reduction **-15%**, Critical DEF **-15%**. Each stack beyond the first inflicts an additional DMG Taken Reduction **-2%** and Critical DEF **-2%**. After being attacked by a rank **1/2/3/rankless** incantation, **2/3/4/1** stacks are added to this status. The existing stacks do not refresh in duration when new stacks are added this way; stacks up to 15 times.
