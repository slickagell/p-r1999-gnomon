Critical Rate **+50%**. When triggering a Bullet effect, retain the final Bullet of that Bullet type, and convert excess Critical Rate into Critical DMG at a **65%** rate; for each type of Bullet, this conversion rate increases by **10%**, up to 5 times. Lasts 3 rounds.
