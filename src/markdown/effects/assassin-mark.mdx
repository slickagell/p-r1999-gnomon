---
title: "Assassin Mark"
code: "ASSASSIN_MARK"
type: "SPECIAL"
isSpecific: "character"
---

When other allies cast an incantation actively, Ezio consumes 1 stack of *\[Assassin Mark]* on the main target to cast rank 1 *\[Work in the Dark]* to them. This attack counts as a follow-up attack and cannot inflict *\[Assassin Mark]* on the target. Stack up to 5 times.
