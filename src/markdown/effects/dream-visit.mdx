---
title: "Dream Visit"
code: "DREAM_VISIT"
type: "POS_STATUS"
isSpecific: "character"
---

Can only cast ultimates and special incantations. The holder's automatically replenished incantations are converted into random incantations of other allies. At the start of each round, gain Moxie +1 and 1 precast incantation _\[Counting Sheep]_.
