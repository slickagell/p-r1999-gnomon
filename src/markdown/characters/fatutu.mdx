  <Fragment slot="skill-breakdown-content">
    Fatutu is a sustain/healer character. Her special *\[Spirit Shell]* effect heals entire team and increases DMG of Extra Action team significantly.

    Fatutu can both deploy amount of *\[Spirit Shell]* stacks to enemy and retrieve amount of *\[Spirit Shell]* stacks from enemy.

    <br />

    *\[Spirit Shell]* effect:

    * Can **not** be purified and has **no** duration.
    * Maximum stacks on both self and enemies is **12**.
    * Effect on self will be **different** from effect on enemies.

    <br />

    :effect{param="spirit-shell-self"} on self:

    * At Insight 1, when entering battle, Fatutu will gain **4** stacks of :effect{param="spirit-shell-self"} for self.
    * With Insight 3, when entering battle, Fatutu will gain total **12** stacks of :effect{param="spirit-shell-self"} for self.
    * When Fatutu is being **attacked** or **sharing damage**, applies **1** stack of :effect{param="spirit-shell"} to the attacker.

    <br />

    :effect{param="spirit-shell"} on enemies:

    * For each stacks on themselves, they will receive an additional **5%** DMG Bonus and **5%** Critical DMG when being attacked with **Follow up Attacks**.
    * When an enemy is being **attacked**, Fatutu will retrieve **1** stack of :effect{param="spirit-shell-self"}.
    * When **multiple** enemies are being **attacked**, Fatutu still retrieve only **1** stack of :effect{param="spirit-shell-self"}.
    * When enemy is being attacked in *\[Dodge]* status, Fatutu still retrieve **1** stack of :effect{param="spirit-shell-self"}.
    * When enemy is **defeated**, Fatutu will retrieve **all** stacks of :effect{param="spirit-shell-self"} on that enemy.

    <br />

    With Insight 1, when deploying :effect{param="spirit-shell"}, Fatutu has a **20%** chance to gain Moxie **+1**.

    Also with Insight 3, when Fatutu exchange (means both deploy and retrieve) *\[Spirit Shell]* for **7** times, triggers a rank 1 *\[Unmendable Cracks]* on the enemy with the most stacks of :effect{param="spirit-shell"} and counted as a **Follow up Attack**.

    Each time a stack is **retrieved**, Fatutu will **heal** the entire team equal to **30%** Fatutu's ATK x number of :effect{param="spirit-shell"} stacks.

    <br />

    With first Debuff skill <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-1" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Unmendable Cracks]*</CharacterSkillTooltip>:

    * Deal single target Mental DMG attack.
    * Deploy **3** stacks of :effect{param="spirit-shell"} to the target.
      * If the enemy already has :effect{param="spirit-shell"} stacks, it means this will deploy **2** additional stacks of :effect{param="spirit-shell"} to the target, since Fatutu will retrieve **1** stack to herself.
    * Dispel **1/2/3** type of *\[Stats Down]*, *\[Neg Status]*, or *\[Control]* effects from all allies.

    <br />

    Second skill <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Ocean in the Shell]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> is a Buff incantation:

    * Retrieve **all** stacks of :effect{param="spirit-shell-self"} on enemies.
    * The entire **HP** of the team will be **divided equally**.
    * Grants **3/5/8** stacks of :effect{param="shared-shells-shared-fate"} to all allies.
      * This effect **can** be dispelled, has **30** maximum stacks and has **no** duration.
      * This effect stacks **can** be reduced even when the character is in *\[Dodge]* status.
      * HP sacrifice by other characters will **not** trigger this effect.
      * If enemy attack multiple allies, :effect{param="spirit-shell"} will also be deployed **multiple** times to the attacker.

    <br />

    Ultimate skill <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="ultimate" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Proverb of the Sea]*</CharacterSkillTooltip>:

    * Deal single target Mental DMG attack.
    * Deploy **all** stacks of :effect{param="spirit-shell"} that Fatutu **has** to the target.
    * Inflict :effect{param="shell-beacon"} on the target for **2** rounds.
      * This effect **can** be dispelled.
      * When attack enemy with this effect, Fatutu will exchange *\[Spirit Shell]* **2** times, 1 retrieve time and 1 redeploy time.

    <br />

    #### Operate / Rotation

    * Cast <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-1" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Unmendable Cracks]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> and <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Ocean in the Shell]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> in the early rounds to gain Moxie. Try to get the Ultimate as soon as possible.
      * Use Debuff <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-1" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Unmendable Cracks]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> first to deploy :effect{param="spirit-shell"} to the target then use the Buff skill <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Ocean in the Shell]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> later.
    * Use <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="ultimate" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Proverb of the Sea]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> Ultimate when the other enemies do **not** have or have **few** *\[Spirit Shell]* so that the amount of *\[Spirit Shell]* can be focused on the target.
      * If *\[Spirit Shell]* lying around between enemies, use <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Ocean in the Shell]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> to retrieve all of *\[Spirit Shell]* **first** then use Ultimate to attack the target later.
      * Use Fatutu's Ultimate **before** using other characters skills.
      * Prioritize <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Ocean in the Shell]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> after using Ultimate.
    * Since Fatutu has a **20%** chance to gain Moxie when deploying :effect{param="spirit-shell"}, depending on the Moxie that Fatutu has on the beginning of each round, we maybe just need to use Debuff or Buff incantation 1-2 times and be able to use Ultimate again.
    * Pay attention to the amount of :effect{param="shared-shells-shared-fate"} stacks and use <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Ocean in the Shell]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> to provide more stacks when it is about to run out.
