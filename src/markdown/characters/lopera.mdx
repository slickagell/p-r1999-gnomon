  <Fragment slot="how-to-play-content">
    Lopera is a character that can buff DMG and healing allies with her mechanism.

    <br />

    With Insight 1, Lopera will gain Moxie +1 and recover (Missing HP x**50%**) HP to the ally with the lowest HP percentage when an attack triggers a *\[Bullet]* effect.

    * This effect will trigger even when Lopera use her first skill <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-1" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Double Backups]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> or do the Follow up Attack: :effect{code="BULL_EYE"} when in channeling status.

    In Insight 3, Lopera gain Moxie +1 and will gain +1 extra Moxie for every other **Beast**, **Spirit**, or **Intellect** ally present up to Moxie +2.

    <br />

    With her first skill <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-1" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Double Backups]*</CharacterSkillTooltip>, deals Reality DMG and convert :effect{code="FLAMING_BULLET"} into 2 other types of *\[Bullet]*:

    * 1 :effect{code="INCANTATION_BULLET"} for allies at the **front** of Lopera.
    * 1 :effect{code="RITUAL_BULLET"} for allies **behind** Lopera.
    * *\[Bullet]* will be given to allies from **left to right**, so if Lopera does not have enough :effect{code="FLAMING_BULLET"}, only the front allies will receive *\[Bullet]*.
    * When activate, Lopera will use 1 :effect{code="FLAMING_BULLET"} first, then convert *\[Bullet]* for allies later. You will need **4** *\[Bullet]* in total, so consider team position.

    <br />

    Using <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Winning Chips]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> grants herself stacks of :effect{code="FLAMING_BULLET"} which can inflict :effect{code="BURN"} and enter channeling status:

    * When in channeling status, at the end of each round, if Lopera has at least 1 *\[Bullet]*, she will cast :effect{code="BULL_EYE"} that deals Reality DMG and **increases Critical DMG for allies** in 2 rounds.
    * If enemy has more than **20** stacks of :effect{code="BURN"}, the buff effect will be **doubled**.
    * :effect{code="BULL_EYE"} will prioritize targets with the highest :effect{code="BURN"}.
    * Channeling status is **permanent** and will end only if Lopera does not have any *\[Bullet]*.
    * If binding with Anjo Nala, Anjo Nala's Ultimate will prevent Lopera from casting :effect{code="BULL_EYE"} at the end of round.

    <br />

    Ultimate <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="ultimate" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[More Than Alchemy]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> will initialize an *\[Array]*: :effect{code="CRUCIBLE_ARSENAL"}

    * *\[Bullet]* status of Lopera and allies will be retained at **1** until the end of *\[Array]*.
    * Even only 1 :effect{code="FLAMING_BULLET"} remained, it still can be converted to :effect{code="INCANTATION_BULLET"}/:effect{code="RITUAL_BULLET"} for allies.
    * Exceed Critical Rate will be converted to Critical DMG, calculated as follow, for example:

    80% <sup>(from character)</sup>  + 40% <sup>(from *\[Crucible Arsenal]*)</sup> = 120% - 100% = 20% <sup>(excess)</sup> \* (40% + 10% \* 2<sup> *\[Bullet]* stacks</sup>) = 12%

    * Characters with **60%** Critical Rate or higher can be exceed when team up with Lopera.

    <br />

    ### Operate / Rotation

    * Use <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Winning Chips]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> first to gain :effect{code="FLAMING_BULLET"} stacks and enter channeling status.
    * Use <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-1" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Double Backups]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> to convert :effect{code="FLAMING_BULLET"} into others *\[Bullet]*, heal allies, and gain Moxie.
    * Cast <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="ultimate" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[More Than Alchemy]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> as much as possible.
