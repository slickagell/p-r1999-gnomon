  <Fragment slot="how-to-play-content">
    Flutterpage is a character that buff Follow-up attack (FuA) characters through the :effect{code="GUST"} effect.

    :effect{code="GUST"} can be gained by:

    * Other allies actions (active or FuA) will grant **1** stack last **3** rounds.
    * Casting <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-1" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Knock, Knock]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> will grant **2** stacks last **3** rounds.
    * **10** stacks of :effect{code="GUST"} last **2** rounds when entering battle when updated to Insight 3

    <br />

    At the beginning of each round, based on the stacks of :effect{code="GUST"} Flutterpage has, she will grant a :effect{code="FORCE_FIELD"} that benefits all allies and the added Reality DMG is considered dealt by Flutterpage herself:

    * Up to **5** stacks or more: gain :effect{code="PAPER_PARADE"}.
    * Up to **15** stacks or more: gain :effect{code="WAVE_OF_SHEETS"}.
    * Up to **25** stacks or more: gain :effect{code="ADIEU_MOTHER_EARTH"}.

    To maintain :effect{code="FORCE_FIELD"} level 3, it is recommended to make allies **do extra actions 2-3 times per round**.

    <br />

    Flutterpage has **8** Eureka in total.

    :effect{code="FORCE_FIELD"} at level 2 and above doesn't need to consume Eureka and still benefits all allies but will lose some DMG.

    With Insight 3, when Flutterpage attacks will grant 2 Eureka to self.

    When in Ultimate channeling, Flutterpage will recover all Eureka at the start of each round.

    <br />

    <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Cotton Kite]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> is a skill that grant a :effect{code="CONTINUOUS_ACTION_I"} effect for **1** round, which takes effect in the following round.

    So prioritize to use this skill to gain Moxie and :effect{code="GUST"} stacks for Flutterpage.

    :effect{code="CONTINUOUS_ACTION_I"} effect is not stackable so **don't use it twice in a round** and **don't combine them** to 2 or 3 stars as it only increases Reality DMG.

    <br />

    Ultimate skill <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="ultimate" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[A Dream That Never Grows Old]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> will recover all Eureka and **convert all incantations** into :effect{code="REGARDS_FROM_THE_SKY"} as a Follow-up Attack at the start of each round.

    At the end of each round, Flutterpage will cast **1** :effect{code="REGARDS_FROM_THE_SKY"} additionally.

    Since this will convert all incantations into :effect{code="REGARDS_FROM_THE_SKY"}, despite 1/2/3 stars, so it **not recommended to combine incantations** of Flutterpage.

    Moxie will not be gained during the Ultimate channeling. At the end of channeling, Moxie will be recovered based on the current level of :effect{code="FORCE_FIELD"}

    <br />

    ### Operate / Rotation

    * Always use <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Cotton Kite]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> when it is in Spelldock, but **only 1 per round**.
    * Use <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="ultimate" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[A Dream That Never Grows Old]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> ultimate  to enter channeling status.
    * <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-1" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Knock, Knock]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> is not prioritized, only use when you need to gain Moxie to Ultimate.
