  <Fragment slot="how-to-play-content">
    Anjo Nala is a character with ability to deal powerful damage through the binding mechanism with other ally.

    <br />

    At the start of battle, Anjo Nala will **choose** 1 ally to bind with, depending on the afflatus of the ally, she will have different buff for her self and binded ally.

    If the ally dies, Anjo Nala will bind with another ally in the next round.

    If only Anjo Nala left, this mechanism will not show up.

    Ally will **not** received Moxie and will not be able to cast ultimates, instead Anjo Nala will gain Moxie.

    Anjo Nala total Moxie will be changed to **12** while Ultimate Moxie cost will be changed to **8**.

    Ultimate of Anjo Nala will trigger Ultimate of binded ally. Both ultimates will be casted at the start of the next round.

    <br />

    With <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-1" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Feast of Lust]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> skill, deal Mental DMG, scale with number of *\[Neg Status]* types on the target, and inflict 1 :effect{code="TITILLATED"}

    * DMG Bonus will **not** add up even if there are 2 or more :effect{code="TITILLATED"} stacks on the target.
    * :effect{code="TITILLATED"} duration will be calculated **independently**, so you will need to inflict 3 stacks within 2 rounds to be able to trigger :effect{code="CAPTIVATED"}

    <br />

    When use <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Twin Phantoms]*</CharacterSkillTooltip>, Anjo Nala will enter *\[Hymn]* status

    * When casting incantation of Anjo Nala in *\[Hymn]* status, it will reduce the duration of *\[Hymn]*.
    * With Insight 3, when binded ally cast active incantations, it will also reduce the duration of *\[Hymn]*.
    * *\[Hymn]* will also reduce by 1 when pass a round.
    * When *\[Hymn]* status end, cast :effect{code="ANGEL_TEMPTATION"}

    <br />

    Ultimate <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="ultimate" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Interwoven Dreams]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> will make both Anjo Nala and binded ally go to *\[Channel]* status and can not act during this round.

    * At the start of next round, the binded ally will lose **50%** of current HP.
    * Ultimate of binded ally will be casted first, then Anjo Nala.
    * If binded ally is in *\[Control]* status, **only** Anjo Nala Ultimate will be casted.
    * If Anjo Nala is in *\[Control]* status, **both** will lose Ultimate.
    * Both Ultimate will be considered as **Follow up Attack**.

    <br />

    ##### Binding effect table

    | Binding             | Buff for Anjo Nala & ally                        | Anjo Nala action when in *\[Hymn]* status (Insight 3)                                              |
    |---------------------|--------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Spirit/Intellect    | ATK and Max HP increased by **4%**               | Casts a rank 1 *\[Feast of Lust]* as a follow-up attack on the enemy with the lowest HP percentage |

    | Mineral/Star        | Critical Rate and Mental DEF increased by **8%** | Casts 1 :effect{code="ANGEL_TEMPTATION"}                                                           |

    <br />

    * If Anjo Nala not in *\[Hymn]* status, try to play <CharacterSkillTooltip variant="skill-2" id={frontmatter.id}>*\[Twin Phantoms]*</CharacterSkillTooltip> first.
    * When Anjo Nala already in *\[Hymn]* status, use the binded ally's incantations.
